Skål

Old Norse  ·  /skɔːl/  · 

Skål is an Old Norse word for a communal drinking bowl and, by extension, the act of sharing that vessel at feasts. Over time it evolved into the familiar Scandinavian toast still heard today — an invitation to drink in camaraderie and good health.

When you raise a glass and say "Skål!" you are echoing a thousand-year-old habit of celebrating togetherness, good drink, and shared prosperity — one simple word capturing both the vessel and the spirit of the toast.

The word also traces a direct line to the English word scale — both vessel and measure — a reminder that the best things in life are worth weighing carefully before you lift the cup.

Built with Django, PostgreSQL, and a deep respect for honey.
