TOSNA 3.0 Schedule (what is this?)

TOSNA 3.0 is a specific protocol for providing nutrients to yeast during the fermentation of mead. It stands for Tailored Organic Staggered Nutrient Addition. This protocol is a refined version of older methods, designed to ensure a clean, consistent, and efficient fermentation process, which helps prevent off-flavors from developing.

Core components and principles

  • Tailored: Uses a calculator to determine nutrients based on:
    • Batch size
    • Original Gravity
    • Yeast strain
    • Potential nutrient contributions from fruit
  • Organic:
    • Fermaid-O: Organic nutrient providing amino acids, vitamins, minerals.
    • Go-Ferm: Used for yeast rehydration, improving viability and reducing lag time.
  • Staggered: Nutrients split into smaller additions over first days.
  • Nutrient Addition: Typically 4 equal additions of Fermaid-O.

Typical TOSNA 3.0 schedule

  • Day 0: Yeast rehydrated with Go-Ferm, then pitched.
  • Day 1 (24h): First Fermaid-O addition.
  • Day 2 (48h): Second addition.
  • Day 3 (72h): Third addition.
  • Day 4/5 (1/3 sugar break or ~96h): Fourth and final addition.

Key differences and benefits

  • TOSNA 2.0 vs 3.0: 3.0 refines yeast/nutrient amounts by strain & OG.
  • TOSNA vs Traditional SNA:
    • Organic vs Inorganic: Fermaid-O vs DAP.
    • Avoiding Off-Flavors: Steady additions reduce yeast stress and fusels.
    • Customization: Tailored to batch variables.
    • Consistency: Prevents sluggish or stuck fermentations.

How These Calculations Work

Specific Gravity is determined by using a hydrometer.

Original gravity (OG) measures sugar before fermentation; final gravity (FG) measures residual sugar after. The difference lets you calculate alcohol.

In winemaking, the "standard" equation for calculating ABV (Alcohol By Volume) is typically used for most situations, while the "alternate" equation is used for higher-gravity wines where greater accuracy is desired.

Basic formula:

ABV = (OG – FG) × 131.25

Alternate formula:

ABV = (76.08 × (OG – FG) / (1.775 – OG)) × (FG / 0.794)

Brix method: ABV = (OB – FB) × 0.55

Attenuation measures sugar converted to alcohol; lower means sweeter.

Calories in grams: (ml × ABV × 8)/1000, then ×7.
