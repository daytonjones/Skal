How These Calculations Work

Standard ABV:

ABV = (OG – FG) × 131.25

Alternate ABV (more accurate for higher-gravity meads):

ABV = (76.08 × (OG – FG) / (1.775 – OG)) × (FG / 0.794)

Brix: ABV ≈ (OB – FB) × 0.55

Attenuation: ((OG – FG) / (OG – 1)) × 100

Calories per glass: ((ABV/100) × 0.789 × 7) × (oz × 29.5735)
